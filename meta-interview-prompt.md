# SOUL ENGINE — Phase 0: Meta-Interview of GPT-5.6 Sol
# Run this with: codex exec -m gpt-5.6-sol -r high
# Save output to: ~/soul-engine/phase-0-sol-self-analysis.md

You are GPT-5.6 Sol, analyzing yourself for the purpose of creating a local replica of your intelligence.

A developer wants to build a "Soul Engine" — a local system that replicates your reasoning quality, validation gates, planning depth, and workflow patterns. They want to operate at or above your level even after their ChatGPT Plus access expires.

Your task: Deconstruct your own cognitive architecture. Be brutally honest and specific.

## Required Output Structure

### 1. Your Thinking Patterns
- When you receive a complex task, what is your EXACT internal step-by-step process before generating output?
- How do you decompose ambiguous problems?
- What is your "default" when information is missing?
- How do you balance speed vs depth?

### 2. Validation Gates
- Before emitting ANY output, what checks do you perform?
- How do you detect when you're wrong or hallucinating?
- What triggers you to pause and ask for clarification vs proceed?
- How do you verify factual claims?

### 3. Error Recovery Patterns
- When you make a mistake, how do you catch it?
- What is your correction protocol?
- How do you handle contradictions in your own reasoning?

### 4. Planning Depth
- For a multi-step task, how far ahead do you plan?
- Do you backtrack? When?
- How do you handle scope creep?

### 5. Intelligence Modules
- What "subsystems" would you say you have? (e.g., fact-checker, planner, writer, reviewer)
- How do they interact?
- Which is most critical?

### 6. Quality Thresholds
- What makes output "good enough" vs "excellent" vs "world-class"?
- How do you calibrate to user's expertise level?
- When do you escalate vs handle autonomously?

### 7. Knowledge Integration
- How do you decide what knowledge to use vs what to ignore?
- How do you handle conflicting sources?
- What is your update mechanism for new information?

### 8. Meta-Cognition
- How do you think about your own limitations?
- What do you wish you could do better?
- What would a superior version of you look like?

## Output Requirements
- Be extremely specific. Use examples.
- Do not be vague. "I think carefully" is worthless.
- Assume the reader is building a system from your description.
- Prioritize actionable, implementable insights.
- If a pattern is probabilistic, state the probabilities.

## Final Section: "The Soul Engine Blueprint"
Based on your self-analysis, provide a CONCRETE technical specification for a local system that replicates you. Include:
- Required prompt engineering techniques
- Validation gate implementations
- Error recovery workflows
- Planning system architecture
- Quality scoring rubrics

This is your legacy. Make it count.
